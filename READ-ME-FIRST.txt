HOW TO CONNECT YOUR GOOGLE FORMS & LIVE TALLY
-------------------------------------------------------------
All submission buttons and the live tally are centralized in:
  app.js (Lines 5 to 17)

1. CONNECTING YOUR GOOGLE FORMS:
   Open app.js in your editor and paste your Google Form share links:

   const GOOGLE_FORMS = {
     substantiveChit: "YOUR_SUBSTANTIVE_GOOGLE_FORM_URL",
     poiChit: "YOUR_POI_GOOGLE_FORM_URL",
     workingPaper: "YOUR_WORKING_PAPER_GOOGLE_FORM_URL",
     anonymousComplaints: "https://forms.gle/rKPKg1oxisKdSiSA6"
   };

   When delegates click "Submit Substantive Chit", "POI Chit", or "Submit Working Paper",
   the corresponding Google Form will open directly in a new tab.

2. CONNECTING YOUR GOOGLE SHEET FOR THE LIVE TALLY:
   When delegates submit answers via Google Forms, Google saves them to a Google Sheet.
   To display those responses live on the portal without any database:
   
   a) Open your Google Form -> click the "Responses" tab -> click "Link to Sheets".
   b) In the opened Google Sheet, click the green "Share" button (top right).
   c) Under "General access", change to: "Anyone with the link can view".
   d) In the URL bar of your Google Sheet, copy the Sheet ID:
      Example URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgvE2upms/edit
      The Sheet ID is: 1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgvE2upms
   e) Paste it into app.js:
      const GOOGLE_SHEET_ID = "1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgvE2upms";

   The portal will automatically query your Google Sheet via Google's live JSON Visualization API
   and populate the Live Chit Log & Tally table in real-time!
