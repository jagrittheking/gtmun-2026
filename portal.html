Delegate Preparation Worksheet

Synthesize your delegation’s national policy, binding international legal precedents, bloc voting alliances, and draft operative clauses before caucus convenes. Notes auto-save continuously to your browser.

💾 Ready · Auto-saves as you type
Section 1

National Stance & Sovereign Interests

What is your delegation’s official position regarding the 17 Non-Self-Governing Territories? Is your government an administering power safeguarding strategic installations (e.g., naval hubs, radar facilities), or an advocate for immediate decolonization, territorial integrity, and the restitution of detached archipelagos?

Section 2

Key International Treaties, Charters & ICJ Precedents

Identify binding legal instruments that substantiate your arguments: UN Charter Chapter XI (Articles 73–74), Resolution 1514 (XV), Resolution 1541 (XV), UNCLOS Part II/V/VI (EEZ and maritime boundaries), and International Court of Justice Advisory Opinions (e.g. Legal Consequences of the Separation of the Chagos Archipelago from Mauritius in 1965).

Section 3

Bloc Allies, Adversaries & Compromise Red Lines

Which regional blocs share your strategic goals (e.g., Non-Aligned Movement, African Union, Pacific Islands Forum, Western European and Others Group)? What are your nation's non-negotiable red lines, and where can you construct pragmatic diplomatic compromises?

Section 4

Proposed Operative Clauses for Working Paper

Draft concrete, actionable clauses using standardized UNGA operative verbs (e.g., Calls upon, Urges, Recommends, Decides, Mandates, Establishes). Focus on verifiable mechanisms such as UN-monitored referendums, joint resource management commissions, or annual compliance audits under the Fourth Committee.
