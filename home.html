START HERE · FOR EVERY DELEGATE

Chit Submissions & Working Papers

In committee, debate moves rapidly. When speaking time is limited or you are not recognised on the General Speakers’ List, chits and written documentation are your most powerful tools to drive policy, challenge opposing delegates, and secure committee marks from the dais.

Remember the golden rule of MUN: a Best Delegate is a very busy delegate. Make every written contribution count.

01 · DIRECT TO DAIS

Substantive Chits

Send arguments, statistics, policy clarifications, or proposed solutions directly to the Executive Board dais. These chits require no addressee, are recorded straight on your committee’s tally, and earn individual scoring marks. Make them argue concrete solutions rather than just quoting facts.

02 · VIA EB TO DELEGATE

POI Chits & Replies

Raise a Point of Information or cross-examine another delegation in writing during formal debate. Select your target country; your question is routed and vetted through the Executive Board dais. Recipient delegates can submit their formal written reply right back via the dais.

03 · BLOC PROPOSALS

Working Papers & Drafts

Draft your bloc's solutions during unmoderated caucuses. When ready, submit your document file or Google Docs link via the committee submission hub. The EB assigns a formal paper number (e.g. WP 1.0) for introduction, floor questions, and formal debate.
